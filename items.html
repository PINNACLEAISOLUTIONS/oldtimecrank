Victor Victrola Model VV-XVIII – Stunning Mahogany Phonograph

$3,250.00

Circa 1915 • Elite Flagship Instrument • Ribbon-Striped Mahogany

Up for sale is a premier piece of musical history: a beautiful Mahogany Victor Model 18 (VV-XVIII) Phonograph. Produced circa 1915, the Model 18 was one of Victor’s most elite flagship instruments, representing the pinnacle of their craftsmanship. These machines were only made for a little over a year and were discontinued after the 1916 Christmas season.

Key Features & Condition: Built with carefully selected, "ribbon-striped" mahogany veneers. The cabinet features elegant serpentine front and sides, accented by classic Louis XV moulding. All original hardware is 24-karat gold plated and remains in very good condition. The motor has been recently serviced and runs smooth and quiet. The reproducer has been rebuilt to ensure excellent playback quality. While refinished several years ago, the machine remains in stunning condition and shows beautifully.

Dimensions: The machine measures 48" high x 23 1/2" wide x 25 1/2" Deep.
